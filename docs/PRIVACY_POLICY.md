# PawnForge Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 3 November 2026**

PawnForge is an offline-first Pawn source editor and compiler for Android.

## Information the app handles

- Source files and project folders that you explicitly open or select are read and edited so the app can provide its editor and compiler features.
- The app stores working copies and preferences in its private application storage.
- The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or telemetry in this build.
- The Android manifest does not request the Internet permission. PawnForge does not send source code, compiler output, or settings to a PawnForge-operated server.

## Files and third-party storage providers

PawnForge uses Android's Storage Access Framework. You choose the files or project folder that the app may access. If you select a document provider that synchronizes with a third-party cloud service, that provider's own privacy terms govern its handling of the selected files.

The app's automatic Android backup is disabled. Keep your own copy of important source files in a location you control.

## Data retention and deletion

Working copies remain in PawnForge's private app storage while the app is installed. You can remove app data or uninstall PawnForge to clear its private working data. Files saved to a folder or document provider that you selected are not deleted when the app is uninstalled; manage those files with that provider.

## Children's privacy

PawnForge is a general-purpose code editor. It does not knowingly collect personal information from users.

## Changes and contact

This notice may be updated when app features or data practices change. For privacy questions, use the publisher contact address shown on the official app listing.

This document describes the current app build. The publisher must host this policy at a stable public URL and keep it consistent with the version submitted to an app store.
